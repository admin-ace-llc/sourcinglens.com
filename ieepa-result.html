📋 What to do next

  1. 1
    Download your CAPE CSV — this file contains your Phase 1 eligible entry numbers formatted exactly as CBP requires.
  2. 2
    Log into ACE Secure Data Portal at ace.cbp.dhs.gov — make sure you're enrolled in ACH for electronic refunds.
  3. 3
    Navigate to the CAPE tab and upload your CSV. CBP typically processes refunds within 60–90 days of acceptance.
  4. 4
    Phase 2 entries — monitor CBP's announcement for Phase 2 CAPE opening. Your broker handoff PDF includes these entries flagged separately.
  5. 5
    Not comfortable filing yourself? Send the Broker Handoff PDF to a licensed customs broker — it includes everything they need to file on your behalf.
